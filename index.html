How this works

  1. Find an unclaimed rock. The ones near the top get closest to Earth, which makes them better gifts.
  2. Pay $5. At checkout you tell us the name you want and who the certificate is for.
  3. You get a printable certificate by email, and this rock's page publicly carries your name until the heat death of the universe or the end of our hosting plan, whichever comes first.

To be completely clear, because people ask: the International Astronomical Union assigns real asteroid names and we have nothing to do with them. Nothing you name here appears in any scientific catalogue. That is the joke. If you want the joke to land, do not tell the recipient it's real.
